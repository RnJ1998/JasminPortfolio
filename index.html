Project: "Makein Kesä" Graphic Design

I created designs for Lappeenranta’s “Makein kesä” campaign in summer of 2024. These were used on VisitLappeenranta’s social media to highlight the different summer offers and deals that the companies part of the campaign were offering.

Project: Lappeenranta TikTok Short Videos

I wanted to see how TikTok videos would perform in VisitLappeenranta’s social media. However, I wasn’t allowed to create VisitLappeenranta’s own TikTok account, so I posted the “Lappeenranta in 6 months” video on IG Reels. I also tested how well the video would take off on my own personal TikTok account. The video gained over 30 000 views on TikTok.

I published the “pov: kesä etelä-karjalassa” video after I noticed that the theme song of the TV show “Karjalan kunnailla” started trending. I made the video by using material that I had gathered previous summer. This time I used more hashtags and the video has gained over 60 000 views. I also noticed that there started to be more “aesthetic” videos focusing on Lappeenranta and South Karelia.

Created a short video in Finnish and in English for the use of Lappeenranta City Orchestra. With the help of a colleague, we filmed and edited a video about where to get tickets, where the concert hall is and other things related to an orchestra experience. The video was published on the city of Lappeenranta and VisitLappeenranta’s social media.
